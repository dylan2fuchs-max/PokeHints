PokeHints Guide

Master the Pokémon word-guessing party game!

🎯 Objective

PokeHints is a multiplayer game where players take turns acting as the Hinter to guide their lobby through a series of secret Pokémon words

👑 Roles & Gameplay

  • The Hinter: Provides clues one word at a time. Can introduce one new word to the grid per clue or reuse unlocked words from previous turns by clicking them.
  • The Guessers: Race to type and submit the correct target word. Each guesser gets one clean guess per box state.

📜 Core Rules & Restrictions

  • 3-Clue Streak Rule: If guessers fail to solve the target word within 3 hints, the Hinter must introduce at least one brand-new word to the 40-box grid on their 3rd clue.
  • No Spoiler Hints: Hinters cannot use any part of the active target word inside their clue phrase.

🔥 Competitive Mode & Timers

When Competitive Mode is enabled (mandatory in public lobbies, optional for private hosts):

  • 30-Second Hinter Timer: Players have 30 seconds to submit a clues and guesses.
  • Hostage Prevention: If the timer expires, the Hinter is heavily penalized (4 boxes burned with `(TIMEOUT)`), all guessers are awarded `+1` point, and the game automatically skips to the next word.
